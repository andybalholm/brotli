Vulnerabilities of LOW or MEDIUM severity should be treated as ordinary bugs,
and reported in our issue tracker. This specifically includes all denial-of-service vulnerabilities.

Vulnerabilities of HIGH or CRITICAL severity should be reported to the maintainer by email: andy@balholm.com.

This security policy is inspired by curl's new policy: https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/10/20/do-minor-curl-vulnerabilities-in-the-open/
